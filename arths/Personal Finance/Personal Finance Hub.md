# 💰 Personal Finance Knowledge Hub

The **Personal Finance Hub** stores statutory rules of thumb, liquidity standards, and budgeting heuristics utilized by the ArthaAI `PersonalFinanceAgent` to provide grounded financial explanations.

---

## 📌 Interconnected Rule Nodes

1. **[[50-30-20 Budgeting Rule]]**
   - Heuristic allocation framework dividing net monthly take-home income into **50% Needs**, **30% Wants**, and **20% Savings/Investments**.
   - Related to: [[Emergency Fund Adequacy]], [[Debt-to-Income Guidelines]].

2. **[[20-4-10 Auto Loan Rule]]**
   - Vehicle purchase affordability heuristic: **20% Down Payment**, **4-Year Tenure Maximum**, and transportation outflows kept under **10% Gross Income**.
   - Related to: [[Debt-to-Income Guidelines]], [[Emergency Fund Adequacy]].

3. **[[Emergency Fund Adequacy]]**
   - Minimum liquid buffer standard recommending **3 to 6 months of essential living expenses** held in high-liquidity instruments (Savings, Overnight Funds, FDs).
   - Related to: [[50-30-20 Budgeting Rule]].

4. **[[Debt-to-Income Guidelines]]**
   - Debt servicing safety thresholds ensuring total monthly loan EMIs do not exceed **35% (Safe)** to **50% (High Risk)** of monthly income.
   - Related to: [[20-4-10 Auto Loan Rule]].

5. **[[Goal Compounding & PMT Heuristics]]**
   - Exact mathematical principles for goal-based investing, future value annuity calculations, and horizon-aligned target contributions.
   - Related to: [[SIP & Compounding Framework]].
