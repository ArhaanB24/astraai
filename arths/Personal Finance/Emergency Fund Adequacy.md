## SECTION: Emergency Fund Coverage Guideline

Financial planners typically recommend maintaining a dedicated liquid emergency buffer equal to **3 to 6 months of essential living expenses** (Housing Rent/EMI, Utilities, Groceries, Insurance Premiums, Debt servicing EMIs):
- **Salaried Individuals (Stable Corporates/Govt)**: 3 to 6 months coverage buffer is standard.
- **Freelancers, Entrepreneurs & Single-Earner Households**: 6 to 12 months coverage buffer is strongly recommended.
- **Deployment Instruments**: Liquid Mutual Funds, High-Yield Savings Accounts, Sweep-in Bank Fixed Deposits.

> [!TIP]
> **IMPORTANT HEURISTIC NOTE**: Emergency fund adequacy is evaluated based on cash-flow stability and liquidity tiers. Never deplete core emergency funds for discretionary purchases or volatile equity down payments.
