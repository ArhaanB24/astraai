## SECTION: Debt-to-Income (DTI) Ratio Guidelines

The **Debt-to-Income (DTI) ratio** measures the percentage of gross monthly income allocated toward servicing recurring debt obligations (Home Loans, Car Loans, Personal Loans, Credit Card EMIs):

$$\text{DTI Ratio (\%)} = \left(\frac{\text{Total Monthly EMIs}}{\text{Gross Monthly Income}}\right) \times 100$$

### Benchmark Tiers:
- **Optimal / Healthy (< 25%)**: Excellent credit profile; ample cash flow surplus for aggressive equity investments and goal accumulation.
- **Moderate / Manageable (25% – 35%)**: Acceptable for homeowners servicing a long-term mortgage alongside reasonable living expenses.
- **Stretched (35% – 50%)**: Heightened financial vulnerability; risk of debt distress during unexpected income disruption.
- **Dangerous (> 50%)**: Unaffordable debt load; immediate debt consolidation or principal prepayment recommended.
