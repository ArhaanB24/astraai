## SECTION: Goal Compounding & PMT Heuristics

Target-oriented goal accumulation requires computing the exact **Required Monthly Contribution (PMT)** by compounding existing corpus and recurring investments against the target horizon ($N$ months) and expected annual rate of return ($r$):

$$FV_{\text{Target}} = PV \times (1 + r_{\text{monthly}})^N + PMT \times \left[\frac{(1 + r_{\text{monthly}})^N - 1}{r_{\text{monthly}}}\right] \times (1 + r_{\text{monthly}})$$

### Strategic Allocation Heuristics by Horizon:
- **Short-Term (< 3 Years)**: Debt Mutual Funds, Bank FDs, Arbitrage Funds (Expected return: 6% – 7.5% p.a.). Minimal equity risk.
- **Medium-Term (3 – 5 Years)**: Conservative Hybrid or Dynamic Asset Allocation Funds (Expected return: 8% – 10% p.a.).
- **Long-Term (> 5 Years)**: Diversified Equity, Flexi Cap & Index Funds (Expected return: 11% – 13% p.a.).
