## SECTION: 50/30/20 Budgeting Rule of Thumb

The **50/30/20 rule** is a popular heuristic budgeting guideline popularized by Senator Elizabeth Warren. It suggests allocating:
- **50% of Net Income** to **Essential Needs** (House Rent, Groceries, Electricity, Utilities, Healthcare).
- **30% of Net Income** to **Discretionary Wants** (Dining out, entertainment, shopping, hobbies, vacations).
- **20% of Net Income** to **Savings, Investments & Debt Repayment** (SIPs, Emergency Fund, Principal debt paydown).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **IMPORTANT HEURISTIC NOTE**: This is a general rule of thumb and not a rigid universal law. Actual allocations depend on city tier (Tier 1 vs Tier 2), income levels, and individual life stage. High-earning households often target savings rates upwards of 40–50%.
