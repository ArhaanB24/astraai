## SECTION: 20/4/10 Auto Purchase Rule of Thumb

The **20/4/10 guideline** is a traditional rule of thumb for automobile financing and purchase affordability:
1. **20% Down Payment**: Pay at least 20% of the vehicle on-road price upfront from non-emergency liquid savings.
2. **4-Year Maximum Loan Tenure**: Finance the vehicle for no longer than 4 years (48 months) to avoid negative equity and excessive cumulative interest outflow.
3. **10% Transportation Budget**: Keep total monthly transportation costs (New Loan EMI + Insurance + Fuel + Maintenance) under 10% of gross monthly income.

> [!NOTE]
> **IMPORTANT HEURISTIC NOTE**: This is a financial guideline to prevent auto debt overextension. In Indian urban contexts, 5-year loan tenures are common, provided total Debt-to-Income (DTI) remains within safe bounds and post-purchase emergency fund reserves remain intact.
