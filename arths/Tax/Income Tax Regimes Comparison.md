# 📊 Income Tax Regimes Comparison

DOCUMENT: Statutory Income Tax Framework

---

## SECTION: FY 2025-26 (AY 2026-27) Statutory Slabs

Under the **Income Tax Act, 1961** (as amended):

### 1. New Tax Regime (Section 115BAC - Default)
- **0 to ₹4,00,000**: Nil (0%)
- **₹4,00,001 to ₹8,00,000**: 5%
- **₹8,00,001 to ₹12,00,000**: 10%
- **₹12,00,001 to ₹16,00,000**: 15%
- **₹16,00,001 to ₹20,00,000**: 20%
- **₹20,00,001 to ₹24,00,000**: 25%
- **Above ₹24,00,000**: 30%
- **Salaried Standard Deduction**: ₹75,000
- **Section 87A Full Rebate Ceiling**: Taxable Income up to **₹12,00,000** (Max rebate **₹60,000** $\rightarrow$ Net tax = **₹0**).
- **Section 87A Marginal Relief**: If Taxable Income marginally exceeds ₹12,00,000, total tax payable before cess is capped at $(\text{Taxable Income} - ₹12,00,000)$.
- **Chapter VI-A Deductions**: **NOT ALLOWED (₹0)** under Section 115BAC(2).

### 2. Old Tax Regime (Optional)
- **0 to ₹2,50,000**: Nil (0%) (₹3.0L for Senior Citizens, ₹5.0L for Super Senior Citizens)
- **₹2,50,001 to ₹5,00,000**: 5%
- **₹5,00,001 to ₹10,00,000**: 20%
- **Above ₹10,00,000**: 30%
- **Salaried Standard Deduction**: ₹50,000
- **Section 87A Rebate**: Taxable Income up to **₹5,00,000** (Max rebate **₹12,500** $\rightarrow$ Net tax = **₹0**).
- **Chapter VI-A Deductions**: **FULLY ELIGIBLE** (Section 80C up to ₹1,50,000; Section 80D up to ₹25,000/₹50,000).
