## SECTION: Section 16(ia) Standard Deduction

Under **Section 16(ia) of the Income Tax Act, 1961**, salaried individuals and pensioners receive a flat statutory deduction from gross salary income without requiring any proof of investment or expenditure:

### Comparative Statutory Limits:
- **New Tax Regime (Section 115BAC)**: **₹75,000** (effective from FY 2024-25 / FY 2025-26 under Finance (No. 2) Act, 2024).
- **Old Tax Regime**: **₹50,000**.

> [!TIP]
> **Zero Proof Required**: The standard deduction applies automatically against gross salary and pension income during payroll TDS computation and annual income tax return filing.
