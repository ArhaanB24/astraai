# 🏛️ Tax Knowledge Hub

Welcome to the **ArthaAI Tax Knowledge Hub**. This domain documents the statutory tax rules, slab structures, deduction frameworks, and rebate mechanisms governing Indian Income Tax under the **Income Tax Act, 1961** and applicable **Finance Acts**.
