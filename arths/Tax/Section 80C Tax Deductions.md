## SECTION: Section 80C & 80CCE Statutory Provisions

Under **Section 80C and Section 80CCE of the Income Tax Act, 1961**, individual taxpayers choosing the **Old Tax Regime** can deduct up to **₹1,50,000 per financial year** from gross total income:

### Eligible Instruments:
1. **ELSS Mutual Funds**: Equity Linked Savings Schemes with a mandatory 3-year lock-in period (shortest among 80C options).
2. **Public Provident Fund (PPF)** & **Employee Provident Fund (EPF)**.
3. **National Savings Certificates (NSC)** & **Tax Saving 5-Year Bank Fixed Deposits**.
4. **Life Insurance Policy Premiums** (Self, Spouse, Children).
5. **Principal Repayment of Housing Loan**.
6. **Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (SSY)**.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Regime Exclusivity**: Section 80C deductions are **strictly available ONLY in the Old Tax Regime**. In the New Tax Regime (Section 115BAC), Chapter VI-A deductions are disallowed in exchange for lower base slab rates.
