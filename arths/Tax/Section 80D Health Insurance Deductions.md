# 🏥 Section 80D Health Insurance Deductions

DOCUMENT: Statutory Income Tax Framework

---

## SECTION: Section 80D Statutory Limits

Under **Section 80D of the Income Tax Act, 1961** (Old Tax Regime), taxpayers can claim deductions for medical insurance premiums and preventive health checkups:

### Deduction Limits:
- **Self, Spouse & Dependent Children**: Up to **₹25,000** (Up to **₹50,000** if self/spouse is a senior citizen $\ge 60$ years).
- **Parents (< 60 years)**: Additional deduction up to **₹25,000**.
- **Senior Citizen Parents ($\ge 60$ years)**: Additional deduction up to **₹50,000**.
- **Preventive Health Checkup**: Up to **₹5,000** within the overall aggregate ceiling.
- **Maximum Potential Deduction**: Up to **₹1,00,000** (if both self/family and parents are senior citizens).

> [!NOTE]
> **New Regime Note**: Section 80D medical deductions cannot be claimed under the New Tax Regime (Section 115BAC).
