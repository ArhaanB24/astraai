## SECTION: Investment Mandate

Axis Bluechip Fund invests predominantly in **Large Cap companies** (top 100 stocks by market capitalization). The fund adopts a quality-focused growth strategy, prioritizing industry leaders with strong balance sheets, pricing power, and high **Return on Equity (ROE)**.

---

## SECTION: Sector Concentration & Risk

The portfolio is heavily concentrated in **Financial Services (39.5%)** and **Information Technology (19.8%)**. Top holdings include **ICICI Bank**, **HDFC Bank**, **Infosys**, and **Reliance Industries**. NAV movements are highly sensitive to banking sector monetary policy announcements and quarterly IT earnings commentary.
