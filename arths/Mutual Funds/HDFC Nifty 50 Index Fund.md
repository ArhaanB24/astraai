## SECTION: Investment Strategy & Index Tracking

The scheme seeks to generate returns that correspond to the total returns of the **Nifty 50 Index**, subject to tracking errors. The fund passively holds all 50 constituents of the Nifty 50 Index in exact market capitalization weights. Major holdings include **HDFC Bank (11.50%)**, **Reliance Industries (9.20%)**, **ICICI Bank (7.80%)**, **Infosys (5.90%)**, and **TCS (4.10%)**.

---

## SECTION: Performance Drivers & NAV Sensitivity

As a passive index fund, NAV movements mirror the broader Indian equity market index (Nifty 50). The tracking error is maintained under **0.05% per annum** with minimal cash drag.
