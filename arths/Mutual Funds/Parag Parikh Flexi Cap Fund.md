## SECTION: Investment Strategy & Asset Allocation

The fund aims to generate long-term capital appreciation by investing in an actively managed portfolio of equity and equity-related securities. The fund maintains flexibility to invest across **Large-Cap, Mid-Cap, and Small-Cap companies in India**, alongside up to **35% exposure to overseas equity securities** (such as Alphabet Inc., Microsoft, Amazon). The fund follows a value-investing strategy focusing on high-quality management, competitive moats, low debt, and attractive cash flow yields.

---

## SECTION: Performance & NAV Movement Drivers

Recent NAV movement in PPFAS Flexi Cap Fund was driven by strong earnings growth in HDFC Bank, Power Grid, and ITC, alongside overseas market momentum in global technology stocks. High cash reserves (approx 12-15%) are held in arbitrage and TREPS to deploy during market corrections.
