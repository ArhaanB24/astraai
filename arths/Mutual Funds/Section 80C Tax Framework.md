## SECTION: Section 80C & ELSS Provisions

Under **Section 80C of the Income Tax Act, 1961** (Old Tax Regime), individual taxpayers can claim deductions up to **₹1,50,000 per financial year** from gross taxable income:

### Comparison of 80C Instruments:
| Instrument | Lock-in Period | Risk Profile | Historical Returns |
|---|---|---|---|
| **ELSS Mutual Funds** | **3 Years (Shortest)** | Equity (Market-Linked) | 12% – 15% CAGR |
| **National Savings Certificate (NSC)** | 5 Years | Sovereign (Guaranteed) | ~7.7% Fixed |
| **Tax Saving Bank FD** | 5 Years | Bank Guarantee | 6.5% – 7.1% Fixed |
| **Public Provident Fund (PPF)** | 15 Years | Sovereign (Guaranteed) | ~7.1% Fixed |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Mandatory Lock-in Rule**: For SIP investments in ELSS schemes, **each monthly installment is subject to its own independent 3-year lock-in** starting from its allotment date.
