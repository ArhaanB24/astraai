## SECTION: Investment Mandate & 80C Lock-in

Axis ELSS Tax Saver Fund is an **Equity Linked Savings Scheme** offering tax deduction benefits under **Section 80C of the Income Tax Act** (Old Tax Regime). Investments are subject to a **mandatory 3-year lock-in period** from the date of each allotment / SIP instalment.

---

## SECTION: Portfolio Composition & Strategy

The fund targets long-term capital appreciation by investing in high-conviction growth stocks across large and mid-cap companies. Key holdings include **ICICI Bank (8.90%)**, **DMart / Avenue Supermarts (5.20%)**, **Bajaj Finance (4.80%)**, **Tata Motors (4.20%)**, and **Pidilite Industries (3.90%)**.
