## SECTION: Portfolio Risk, Concentration & Overlap

Holding multiple mutual funds often creates an illusion of diversification while concentrating risk into identical underlying bluechip stocks and industrial sectors:

### 1. Stock & Sector Overlap
- When an investor simultaneously holds a Flexi Cap fund and an Index fund, both schemes allocate heavy weights to common securities like **HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, Infosys, and Reliance Industries**.
- True portfolio overlap percentage is calculated by summing the minimum common weights:
  $$\text{Overlap}_{A, B} = \sum_{k} \min\left(w_{A, k}, w_{B, k}\right)$$

### 2. Risk-Adjusted Return Metrics
- **Sharpe Ratio**: Excess return generated per unit of total standard deviation volatility.
- **Sortino Ratio**: Excess return per unit of **downside semivariance** (penalizes only harmful negative volatility).
- **Maximum Drawdown (MDD)**: Peak-to-trough peak loss percentage during market drawdowns.
